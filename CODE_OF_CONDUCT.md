# Code of conduct

Be respectful, specific and constructive. Discuss the work and its behavior;
avoid personal attacks. Welcome questions and different levels of experience.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, unwanted sexual attention and sharing
someone else's private information are not acceptable in project spaces.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, limit participation, or block an
account to address misconduct. Decisions should be proportionate to the behavior
and its impact.

To raise a concern, contact a maintainer using a contact route on their public
GitHub profile. If no private route is available, open a minimal issue requesting
one without naming affected people or publishing incident details. Maintainers
should keep reports private except where sharing is needed to resolve them.
